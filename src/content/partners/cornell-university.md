Cornell University offers countless ways for students to make the most of their college experience, encouraging exploration while providing the resources to achieve their goals. The academic culture is energetic and collaborative, with challenging coursework across nearly 80 majors, world renowned faculty, extensive research and experiential learning opportunities, and a focus on solving real-world problems. Community is also at the center of the Cornell experience; students can make connections by joining some of the university's 1000+ student organizations, cheering on Big Red Athletics, and getting involved with community engagement initiatives. Beyond campus, Ithaca, NY, is a vibrant college town that’s consistently ranked as one of the most beautiful in the nation.
