Located in Durham, North Carolina, Duke University is a leading research institution known for academic excellence, vibrant community, and inclusive excellence. Students from around the world come to Duke to learn from world-class faculty and address complex challenges through discovery, service, and innovation.

Duke’s support of STORY reflects our own history. Founded as Brown’s Schoolhouse in rural Randolph County, North Carolina, Duke became Trinity College before relocating to the then-small town of Durham and eventually becoming Duke University. This history gives us a deep appreciation for the talent, resilience, and perspectives found in rural and small-town communities.

Today, Duke is a global university rooted in the South. We are committed to expanding access for students across the Carolinas and beyond, affirming that exceptional talent exists everywhere and geography should never limit opportunity.
