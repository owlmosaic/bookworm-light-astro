Smith is a world-class women’s liberal arts and sciences college located in the vibrant college town of Northampton, Massachusetts. We offer 50 majors, including engineering, and our open curriculum gives you the freedom to explore your passions across multiple disciplines. We guarantee free tuition for students from families earning up to $150,000 annually (with typical assets) and meet full demonstrated need without loans. Students also have access to fellowships, internship funding, and guaranteed housing. Our students come from across the country and around the world, creating an engaging and supportive community unlike any other. With our undergraduate focus, you will be your professors’ top priority in class and in research. After four years, you will graduate a more powerful version of yourself, confident in your ability to make an impact in the world.
