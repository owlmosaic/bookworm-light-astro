Stanford University is a place of openness, exploration, and possibility. Since our opening in 1891, Stanford has been dedicated to finding solutions to big challenges and to preparing students for leadership and engaged citizenship in the world. Stanford students come from across the United States and throughout the world, representing a wide variety of academic interests, belief systems, experiences, backgrounds, and perspectives. We welcome small town and rural students and value their immense contributions to our community.

Some of our relevant programs and resources include:

- Outreach efforts to high schools and community-based organizations in small towns and rural areas.
- Dedicated programming that demystifies the college application process.
- Financial aid to meet the full demonstrated need of all admitted students, without loans.
- Campus organizations such as the Stanford Rural Engagement Network, Community Centers, and the O’Donohue Family Stanford Educational Farm.
