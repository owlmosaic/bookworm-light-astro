The University of Notre Dame is the premier global Catholic research university. Inspired by the University's mission to be a force for good in the world, students pursue a top-tier education, groundbreaking research, and community service during their time on campus. Founded in 1842, Notre Dame is ranked among the top 20 national institutions of higher learning. Here you will find the resources to explore your passions, develop your talents, and live with purpose in a tight knit and supportive community. Students work alongside leading experts to turn ideas into action through research, internships and creative projects that extend beyond the classroom. This emphasis on experiential learning and strong alumni network sets Notre Dame students up for success: graduates have a 98% placement rate in the workforce, graduate school, military, or service. Most notably, Notre Dame strives to be an accessible and affordable institution for all students by offering need-blind admissions and meeting students' full demonstrated needs without loans.
