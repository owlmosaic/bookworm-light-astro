At WashU, you will join a community of achievers who inspire each other with their creativity and tenacity. You’ll acquire the knowledge, confidence, and skills to succeed as a professional and as a person. You’ll learn from faculty leading their fields, and you’ll have the opportunity to work with them on research projects. We are committed to making a WashU education affordable. We meet 100% of demonstrated need for all admitted undergraduate students without loans. With more than 400 student-led clubs and organizations, you’ll have plenty of opportunities to get involved while having fun and building lifelong connections.
